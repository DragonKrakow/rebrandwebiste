Insurance guidance
Independent specialist medical travel insurance may be available to eligible patients; coverage must be verified.

What about insurance?

Standard travel insurance may not cover complications from planned dental procedures. Ask for written confirmation from your insurer about procedures, destination, exclusions and claims.

Prototype notice: SmileSkopje has not confirmed an insurance partnership or insurance-backed treatment guarantee. Do not advertise coverage until contracts and policy wording are verified.
